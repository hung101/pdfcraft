Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate PDF pages offline?
Drop your PDF file into the rotator, select rotation angles for specific pages, and click Rotate. The metadata updates instantly in your browser tab.
Does rotating reduce the quality of the PDF images?
No. PDFCraft changes the rotation metadata tag in the PDF structure. The underlying images are not re-compressed, keeping quality exactly 100% identical.
Can I rotate only a single page in a large document?
Yes, you can rotate specific pages individually without affecting the rotation of other pages.
Is this tool safe for medical records or bank statements?
PDFCraft processes all files locally on your device, so no third-party processor receives your document. That removes the external-transfer risk, though it is not a compliance certification.

Rotation is one number, not a redraw

Every PDF page carries an optional entry in its dictionary called /Rotate, holding 0, 90, 180 or 270. Rotating a page here changes that number and nothing else. The content stream — the actual drawing instructions for text, lines and images — is not touched, re-encoded, or regenerated.

This is why rotation is instantaneous even on a very large document, and why it is perfectly lossless. A 400-page scanned file rotates as fast as a one-page memo, because the work is editing a handful of integers rather than processing any page imagery.

What rotation can and cannot fix

  • Only right angles. The PDF specification allows 0, 90, 180 and 270 degrees. There is no valid way to store a 7-degree rotation in /Rotate.
  • It will not straighten a skewed scan. A page photographed slightly crooked needs the imagery itself resampled, which is a different operation and a lossy one.
  • A few readers ignore it. Rotation is an instruction to the viewer. Well-behaved software honours it; the occasional old or minimal PDF renderer does not.
  • Printing follows the viewer. Because the flag is applied at display time, how a rotated page prints depends on the printing application respecting it too.

Rotating selectively

Does rotating reduce the quality of scanned pages?
Not at all. No pixels are read, resampled or re-compressed. The stored image is identical before and after; only the instruction telling the viewer which way up to present it changes.
Can I rotate some pages and leave the rest alone?
Yes. Rotation is stored per page, so a document can legitimately contain portrait and landscape pages at different orientations. Select only the pages you want turned.
Why does my page look rotated in one app but not another?
That application is ignoring the /Rotate entry. It is rare in current software, but if you need the orientation to be unconditional, the page imagery has to be physically re-rendered instead.
